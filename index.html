Ike Observer Archive is a personal desktop companion for recording selected camera and microphone sessions, keeping searchable transcripts, and archiving recordings in the owner’s Google Drive.

01 / CAPTURE

Choose when to record.

Start an observer session explicitly. Save timestamped camera, microphone, and companion speech records. Stop controls end capture.

02 / REMEMBER

Return to the context.

Local speech recognition creates transcripts. Mempherust indexes recording references so an agent can help locate earlier interactions.

03 / ARCHIVE

Keep a durable copy.

Recordings are packaged into ZIP files, uploaded to the owner’s Google Drive through rclone, and verified before eligible local originals are removed.

Why the app asks for Google Drive access

The desktop application uses Google OAuth to upload archive ZIPs, inspect archive metadata, verify uploads, and download recordings when requested. Drive access supports the owner’s archive workflow; this website has no Google sign-in or access to that data.

Built for personal use

This is an owner-operated application, not a public recording or storage service. Local copies normally expire after 30 days without use; low disk space can trigger earlier cleanup of verified archived copies. Remote ZIP archives are retained until the owner removes them.

Recording and agent sharing are separate choices. Selected conversation text and images may be sent to the configured agent provider. See the privacy policy for how capture, storage, sharing, and deletion work.
