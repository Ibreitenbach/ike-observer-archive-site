Data and control

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Ike Observer Archive is personal software operated by Ike Breitenbach. This policy describes the desktop companion and its Google Drive archive integration, as well as this public information website.

Information the application processes

When the owner starts an observer session, the application can record microphone audio and optionally camera video. It also records companion-generated speech and playback events. Records may include transcripts, timestamps, session and agent identifiers, file hashes, and archive locations. Microphone transcription and speech synthesis run locally.

Google account and Drive access

The owner authorizes rclone through Google OAuth. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the owner’s local configuration, not in this public website or repository. The configured Drive scope can grant broad file access; the archive workflow uses it to upload ZIP archives, list and inspect archive metadata, verify uploaded data, and retrieve archive files. Google Drive hosts uploaded recordings and their provenance manifests.

The application does not use Google Drive data for advertising, sell it, or use it to train a generalized AI model. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Memory and agent services

Explicit observer sessions enable transcript indexing in Mempherust and metadata records in the local Silent Observer audit service. Recording content is evidence, not authorization to execute commands. When the owner interacts with a connected coding agent, selected messages, memory excerpts, and sampled images may be transmitted to that agent’s provider, including OpenAI for Codex. Material retrieved from the archive may likewise be supplied to an agent when used for a requested task. Those providers handle transmitted data under their applicable account settings and policies.

Retention and deletion

Local recordings become eligible for archival after 30 days without use or when disk space is low. Archive uploads are verified before eligible originals are removed. Downloaded ZIPs and extracted recordings are normally removed locally after 30 days without use; use through the companion recall tools resets the relevant timer. The low-space safeguard can remove safely archived local copies earlier.

Remote ZIPs are not automatically deleted. Local indexes and Mempherust transcript records remain separately available for recall. Removing a local media file does not remove its Drive archive, memory record, audit metadata, or an agent provider’s conversation history. The owner must manage those copies separately using the relevant application or service.

Controls and access

Capture begins through explicit controls. Stop controls end recording; the browser implementation also stops capture when the tab is hidden or disconnected. The owner can revoke the Google connection through Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents future authorized Drive operations but does not delete existing archives. The owner can delete archives directly in Google Drive.

This website

This site has no recording controls, login form, application telemetry, advertising, or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process normal technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. No recordings, transcripts, OAuth credentials, or private archive indexes are published here.

Contact and changes

For questions about this application or policy, use the contact page. Do not submit private recordings, transcripts, or credentials in a public issue. Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised effective date.
