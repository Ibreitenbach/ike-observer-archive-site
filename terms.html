Purpose and access

The application provides user-controlled recording, transcript indexing, coding-agent interaction, and Google Drive archival for personal use. This website describes the application; it does not offer public account registration, recording, or hosted storage.

Authorized use

Use only accounts, devices, files, and recordings you are authorized to access. You are responsible for obtaining permissions required for recording or sharing other people’s interactions and for complying with applicable laws. Do not use the application to gain unauthorized access, secretly monitor people without required permission, or infringe others’ rights.

Your accounts and content

You retain your rights in your content. You control which sessions to record and which material to share with connected agents. Protect your machine, OAuth credentials, tokens, and connected accounts. Connecting Google Drive authorizes the configured archive operations under the permissions you grant. Google and other connected services remain subject to their own terms and account settings.

Storage and deletion

Archive uploads are verified before eligible local originals are removed. Local copies normally expire after 30 days without use, and disk-pressure safeguards can remove safely archived copies earlier. Remote ZIP archives remain until you remove them. Deleting a local recording or revoking Google access does not delete existing Drive archives, memory indexes, audit records, or agent-provider conversation history. See the privacy policy for details.

Accuracy and availability

The application is under active development. Recordings may have gaps, transcription and agent responses may contain errors, and uploads or retrieval may fail because of connectivity, quotas, or service changes. Review consequential outputs and maintain independent backups of irreplaceable material. The application is not an emergency monitoring service, and uninterrupted capture or availability is not promised.

Stopping use

You can stop capture through the application controls, stop running the software, and revoke its Google access through Google Account connections. Manage stored copies separately in their respective locations.
